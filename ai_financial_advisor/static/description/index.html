AI Financial Advisor & Position Report

Turn live Odoo Accounting data into a boardroom-ready financial position report — Balance Sheet, Income Statement, KPIs — then let the AI provider of your choice turn those numbers into an executive summary, a financial health score, and specific, numbered recommendations.

✦ Claude ✦ ChatGPT ✦ Gemini ✦ DeepSeek ✦ Grok

Key features

📊 Built from real ledger data

Every figure is pulled directly from posted journal entries for the period and company you choose — no manual data entry, no spreadsheets to reconcile.
🤖 Bring your own AI provider

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Grok are supported out of the box. Switch providers per report to compare how each one reads the same numbers.
🏷️ Admin-managed model catalog

When a vendor ships a new model, add it from the UI in seconds — no module update or code change required, ever.
🚩 Risk alerts, not just praise

The AI is explicitly prompted to surface data-integrity issues and financial red flags in their own dedicated section, separate from general advice.

How it works

Eight steps, from first-time setup to a finished, exportable analysis.

  1. Configure your AI providers once, in Settings — under Accounting › Configuration › Settings › AI Financial Advisor, pick a default provider, drop in each API key, and click Test Connection to confirm it works.
  2. Maintain your own AI Model Catalog — add a brand-new model the day it is released, mark one as default per provider, and archive old ones — all from the UI, never hardcoded.
  3. Create a report and choose your provider & model — reports keep a running history with date range, company, provider, and status.
  4. Click Compute Financials — the full Balance Sheet populates instantly: Cash, Receivables, Inventory, Fixed Assets, Payables and Equity, automatically balanced.
  5. Drill into expense/revenue breakdowns and top suppliers — automatically ranked by spend, and used to ground the AI's recommendations in your real numbers.
  6. Run AI Analysis — get a 0-100 Financial Health Score in seconds, with a plain-language rating (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
  7. Read specific, numbered recommendations — Income Increase, Expense Reduction, Purchasing Optimization, and a dedicated Risk Alerts section, referencing your real accounts and figures.
  8. Export the whole analysis as a branded PDF — your company letterhead and logo, Health Score, Balance Sheet, Income Statement, KPIs, and the full AI analysis, ready for a board pack.

Why choose this module

  • No vendor lock-in — use whichever AI provider your organization already trusts, or compare several side by side.
  • No hardcoded model lists — the AI Model Catalog means new models are a data entry, not a code release.
  • Data stays in your ledger — every number is computed live from your own posted journal entries.
  • Multi-company aware and per-report auditable — every report keeps its own inputs, provider, model, and AI output.
  • Exportable — print any analysis straight to PDF for board packs or audit files.
